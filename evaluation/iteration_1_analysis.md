## Generalizable failure modes

1. Selected the year with the largest raw count of clashes even when the asked relative mapped to a different pillar.
2. Treated the day branch only as spouse palace; missed that it can also be the native's body/life interface.
3. Solved five questions for the same person independently and lost chronology constraints.
4. Over-selected dramatic options without enough multi-year evidence.
5. Used elemental temperament and visible peer stars too directly for personality.
6. Underused age, place, historical education access and other legitimate base-rate constraints.

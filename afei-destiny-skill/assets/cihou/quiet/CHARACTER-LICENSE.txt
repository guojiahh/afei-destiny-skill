The source's 20 Walk PNG frames are packed without creative changes into
 girl-walk.png. The original frame canvas is preserved to retain the artist's
 gait, hair and dress animation. The source archive and atlas hashes are
 recorded in girl-walk-source.json. Runtime scaling and facing are applied
 by the report renderer. No online access is needed to play the animation.
